    Postmodernist study / original HTML and CSS

    B / room for a detour

    Reference: Martine Bedin, Super lamp prototype (1981), V&A M.1-2011. The coach inspected its actual image: blue semicircle, wheels and radial colored bulb holders combine lamp and toy. V&A source.

    A round field interrupts a square product mat; offset forms suggest a small detour. An italic serif headline beside plain sans-serif controls mixes expressive and functional voices. The same restrained palette as A keeps the contrast about composition/type, not adding more colors.

    Intended effect: Suggest small, friendly discoveries while keeping the white tee and next action legible. Risk: Offset geometry and mixed type may distract from price and size selection.

    Audience effect is a hypothesis, not research. The product, price, headline, support and action match the other study. Compare both.
