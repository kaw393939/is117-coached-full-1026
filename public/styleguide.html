00 / Direction

Small discoveries.

A simple base for small discoveries with friends. Audience needs remain hypotheses, not customer research.

Chosen direction / coached simulation

Audience

Class → café

For a student meeting friends after class: casual, but put together. Leave room for a second visitor’s colorful jacket.

Archetype

Explorer

The brand invites new cafés with friends, rather than big adventures. It does not classify its customers.

Visual direction

Clear + bold

Direction A’s grid and bold sans-serif keep the headline easy to read on a phone. See both studies.

06 / Voice

Words are
design, too.

Curious, casual, welcoming. Familiar everyday plans invite a connection; useful styling help stays freely available.

Curious + casual

“Class done. Café next?”

Familiar after-class plans express the Explorer character in everyday terms. Liking is a design hypothesis, not a claim of customer approval.

Welcoming + useful

“Try your white tee with jeans and an open overshirt for a café stop with friends.”

The optional free outfit idea offers help before any action: a reciprocity application. No signup or purchase is required, and no product property or conversion result is claimed.

A living reference. Change the shared tokens and logo, then compare this board with the storefront at the same width. Check the logo, typography, components, color roles, and voice text; sharing a stylesheet alone does not keep every example current. Save the reason for your choices in SPEC.
